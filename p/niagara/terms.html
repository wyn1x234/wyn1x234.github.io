Terms of Use

Last updated: 26 November 2026

These Terms govern the use of this website and the weekday morning shuttle we sell through it. By making a booking you accept them. If you do not accept them, please do not use the site or the service.

1. Who we are

The service is operated by Upper Canada Express, 4761 Crysler Avenue, Niagara Falls, ON L2E 3V9, Canada ("we", "us"). Vehicles and drivers are supplied by Niagara Falls Taxi, a licensed local operator.

2. Booking

There is one morning run on business days: Niagara Falls at 06:45, St. Catharines at 07:05, Burlington GO between 07:55 and 08:05. No other departures are sold on this website.

Booking opens three days ahead and closes at 18:45 the evening before departure. A submitted form is a booking request; it becomes a booking when we confirm it by email to the address you gave us. No account is needed, and an email address is the only contact detail we require. A phone number is optional.

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you send us: the date, the pickup point, the number of seats and any special requirement. Arrive at the pickup point a few minutes early — the van cannot wait, because that would delay every other passenger and the train connection.

3. Fares

The fare depends on how many people travel and is listed on the home page: $34.90 per seat when seven travel, $39.90 at six, $49.90 at four to five, and $59.90 at three. All prices are in Canadian dollars and include applicable taxes.

The fare shown when you book is your maximum. At 18:45 we see how full the van is, and your seat price can only fall from the amount you were shown. It cannot rise.

The GO train from Burlington is a separate service. Its ticket is not included in our fare and must be bought from Metrolinx.

4. Payment

This website does not take payments and does not store card details. Your booking request records the seats and the fare; payment is arranged with the operator and confirmed in the email we send you before the ride. The seats are held for you from the moment we confirm them.

5. Cancellation and no-show

  • More than 24 hours before departure: no charge, and any payment returned in full.
  • Between 24 and 12 hours before departure: we keep 80% of the fare, because the seat can no longer be sold at the usual price.
  • Less than 12 hours before departure, or not showing up: the full fare is due.

Cancel in writing, by replying to the confirmation email. The time we receive your message is the time that counts.

If we cancel the run — a breakdown, a road closure, a driver falling ill — you pay nothing, and any amount already paid is returned in full or credited to another date, as you prefer. We are not responsible for costs you booked elsewhere, such as train tickets or work arrangements.

6. What travels with you

Up to four large or eight small bags fit in the van; tell us in the booking note if you carry more. Children travel from the age of ten, quiet, and no more than one child per adult — younger children need a private car for now. Pets travel in a carrier only; without one the driver may refuse the ride and the fare stays due. Wheelchair-accessible minivans are part of the fleet: they take fewer regular seats, so book a day ahead where you can.

7. Behaviour on board

Follow the driver's reasonable instructions and the rules of the road. A passenger who is aggressive, seriously intoxicated or otherwise not fit to travel may be removed from the run before departure, under the terms accepted at booking. In that case the fare is not refunded, and we may decline future bookings from the same person.

Smoking, alcohol and hot food are not allowed in the vehicle.

8. Timekeeping

Arrival times at Burlington GO are estimates. Traffic, weather and border-region roadworks are outside our control, so we cannot guarantee a particular train. If the 08:12 connection is missed, the next one usually leaves at 08:43. Missing a train because of a delay is not a ground for a refund of the fare, but we will tell you honestly what happened.

We are not liable for indirect losses — a missed shift, a lost opportunity, a separate ticket — or for losses caused by circumstances beyond our reasonable control: weather, road closures, police or border instructions, or a third party's acts.

Nothing in these Terms limits rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2002 (Ontario) or any other law that cannot be excluded.

10. Your personal information

We use the email address, and the phone number if you give one, to confirm your seat, to tell you about changes to the run, and to answer your message. We do not sell your details and do not use them for advertising.

Forms on this website are delivered to our inbox by a third-party form service (FormSubmit). Booking details are kept only as long as needed for the ride and for our accounting obligations. You may ask us to correct or delete your information by writing to the address in section 12. We handle personal information in line with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (Canada).
