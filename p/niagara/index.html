Before you book

Before you travel.

Luggage

Each van can accommodate up to four large bags or eight small bags. If you need to bring additional luggage, please mention it in your booking notes.

Children

Children must be at least 10 years old and accompanied by an adult. A maximum of one child per adult is permitted. Children under 10 can't use this shared service for now and will need private transportation.

Pets

Pets are welcome only when transported in an appropriate carrier. If your pet isn't in a carrier, the driver may refuse transportation, and the fare will still be charged.

Accessibility

Wheelchair-accessible minivans are available. As these vehicles have reduced passenger capacity, we recommend booking at least one day in advance.

Fares and cancellations

The fare displayed when you book is the maximum amount you'll pay. At 6:45 PM we finalise the passenger groups and arrange the vehicles. If additional passengers join, your fare will be reduced accordingly. It will never increase.

We organise the passenger groups ourselves to make sure everyone with a confirmed booking has a seat. We don't display live passenger numbers throughout the day. Instead, we finalise the groups before departure so everyone can travel as planned.

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before departure. Cancellations made between 12 and 24 hours before departure incur an 80% charge. Cancellations made less than 12 hours before departure are charged in full. This policy helps us keep fares predictable and affordable for the other passengers.

We reserve the right to refuse transportation to passengers who are not fit to travel, in accordance with the terms and conditions accepted at booking. In such cases, the fare is non-refundable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Send us a message below, and we'll get back to you the same day.

How far in advance can I book? When do bookings close?

Bookings open three days ahead and close at 6:45 PM the evening before departure. At that time, we'll confirm your ride, final passenger numbers, and your fare.

Do I need an account?

No. All you need is an email address — that's where the confirmation goes. A phone number is optional if you'd rather get a text. There's no need to create an account or remember another password.

Why does my booking initially show only three passengers?

Before bookings close, we display an initial group of three passengers, including yourself. At 6:45 PM we finalise the groups and arrange the vans, and your final fare may be lower than the amount originally displayed — but it will never be higher.

When will my card be charged?

Your card is authorised when you book to secure your seat. The actual payment is processed after your ride. If additional passengers join your group, you'll be charged the lower fare.

Why might passengers pay different fares?

Each passenger has their own maximum fare, based on the price shown when they book. Their fare can only decrease, never increase.

What if I miss the 8:12 AM train?

Our target arrival time at Burlington GO is 8:05 AM. If traffic causes us to miss the 8:12 AM train, the next available train departs at 8:43 AM.

Who operates the service, and is the ride insured?

Niagara Falls Taxi, a local company with nearly 50 years of experience. The vehicles accommodate up to seven passengers and are covered by $5 million in commercial liability insurance. Wheelchair-accessible minivans are available.

Can I bring a suitcase and a laptop bag?

Yes, within the luggage limits: up to four large or eight small bags per van. Please mention any additional luggage in your booking notes.

Is the service available on weekends?

Our regular service operates on weekday mornings. Weekend and holiday rides can be arranged upon request.

Do you offer discounts for regular commuters?

Yes — commuter discounts start at five rides per month. Complete the feedback form with your email address, and we'll send you the commuter discount details along with a 20% discount code for your first ride.
