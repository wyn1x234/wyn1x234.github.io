Before you book

What fits in the van.

Luggage

Up to 4 large or 8 small bags in the van. Tell us in the booking note if you carry more.

Children

From 10 years old, quiet, and no more than one child per adult. Younger children need a private car for now.

Pets

In a carrier only. Without a carrier the driver can refuse the ride and the fare stays charged.

Accessibility

Wheelchair-accessible minivans are part of the fleet. They take fewer regular seats, so book a day ahead when you can.

Fares and cancellations

Your seat price is fixed at the moment of booking and can only fall. If fewer people travel than expected, you still pay no more than your cap — the difference is ours, never yours.

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before departure. Between 24 and 12 hours we keep 80% of the fare, under 12 hours — the full fare. This is what keeps other passengers' prices from rising when someone drops out.

A passenger who is not fit to travel can be removed from the run under the terms accepted at booking. The fare is not refunded in that case.

FAQ

Questions before the first ride.

Anything else — write to us below, we answer the same day.

When can I book, and when does booking close?

Bookings open three days ahead and close 12 hours before departure — that is 18:45 the evening before an 06:45 run.

When is my card charged?

We authorise the fare at booking to hold your seat, and charge it after the ride. If more passengers join and the fare drops, we charge the lower amount.

Why do other passengers pay a different fare?

The fare depends on how many people travel. Everyone's price is locked at their own booking, so it can never rise afterwards — only fall.

What if I miss the 08:12 train?

We aim to reach Burlington GO by 08:05. If traffic delays the van and you miss the 08:12 connection, the next train leaves at 08:43.

Who drives, and is the ride insured?

Niagara Falls Taxi, a local company operating for almost 50 years. Vehicles carry up to 7 seats and $5M business liability insurance. Wheelchair-accessible minivans are available.

Can I bring a suitcase and a laptop bag?

Yes, within the luggage limits: up to 4 large or 8 small bags in the van. Add a note at booking if you carry more.

Do you run at weekends?

Weekday mornings are the regular service. Weekend and holiday trips are available on request.

Is there a discount for regular commuters?

Yes — commuter discounts start from 5 rides a month. Leave your email in the feedback form and we will send the terms plus a 20% coupon for your first ride.
